Overview

This project implements a reinforcement learning-based interception system in a custom 2D simulation. An attacking rocket attempts to reach a protected area while a defensive rocket, controlled by a Deep Q-Network (DQN) agent, learns to intercept it.

The environment, reward structure, and action space were implemented from scratch. Each episode randomizes the attacker's position, orientation, and speed to ensure robust generalization.
